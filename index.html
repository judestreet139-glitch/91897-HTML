NieR:Automata & NieR:Replicant

Automata:

Earth was invaded by aliens, forcing surviving humans to flee to a lunar base. Humanity sends elite android soldiers of the YoRHa squad—primarily characters 2B, 9S, and A2—to reclaim the planet.

Replicant:

Set in a decaying, apocalyptic future where human civilization has regressed to medieval conditions, the story follows a kind young man. He embarks on a desperate quest to find a cure for his sister, Yonah, who is terminally ill with a mysterious disease called the Black Scrawl. Alongside a strange, talking magical book named Grimoire Weiss, he searches for the "Sealed verses" to save his sister, encountering unusual allies and dangerous shadow-like monsters known as shades along the way. The narrative explores themes of sacrifice, the nature of humanity, and the consequences of one's actions in a world filled with moral ambiguity.

Explore a description of lore showcasing the characters, locations, and memorable moments from NieR:Automata & NieR:Replicant.

The story of NieR spans thousands of years and connects the worlds of Drakengard, NieR Replicant and NieR:Automata. What begins with a supernatural event eventually leads to the destruction of humanity, the creation of Replicants and Gestalts, and the endless conflict between Androids and Machines.

Drakengard 3

Long before the events of NieR, the world was shaped by a mysterious woman known as Zero. Zero was one of the Intoners, five sisters who possessed extraordinary powers connected to singing. The Intoners were created through Zero's attempt to destroy a supernatural flower that had infected her. The flower was a parasitic entity capable of granting immense power while eventually consuming its host.

Zero eventually became determined to kill her own sisters before they could spread their influence throughout the world. However, the Intoners became powerful rulers, each controlling their own territory. Zero created a dragon named Michael to help her fight them.

The events of Drakengard 3 follow Zero as she travels across the world hunting down the other Intoners. Her journey eventually leads to a confrontation with One, the youngest and most powerful of the Intoners.

Zero's dragon Michael eventually dies, forcing her to find another dragon. His reincarnation, Mikhail, becomes Zero's new companion. Although Mikhail appears innocent and childlike, he possesses enormous power and becomes deeply attached to Zero.

The events surrounding Zero and the Intoners eventually lead into the complicated branching timelines of Drakengard. One important consequence is the creation of Accord, an organisation that observes different timelines and records events across the world.

Drakengard

Centuries later, the world of Drakengard follows Caim, a young prince whose kingdom is attacked during a brutal conflict. During the attack, Caim's parents are killed and he is seriously wounded.

Near death, Caim encounters a dying dragon. In order to survive, the two form a pact. Caim sacrifices his ability to speak in exchange for the dragon's power, creating a supernatural bond between human and dragon.

Caim eventually becomes involved in a massive war involving the Union, the Empire and the mysterious Grotesquerie Queen. The world is filled with supernatural creatures, magical weapons and beings whose existence is connected to powerful forces beyond humanity.

Caim is eventually joined by several companions, including the dragon Angelus. Their journey gradually reveals that the conflict is much larger than a normal war.

The Grotesquerie Queen possesses a supernatural power that threatens the stability of the world itself. Drakengard contains several possible endings, but one particular ending becomes extremely important to the history of NieR.

Drakengard Ending E

In Drakengard's fifth ending, Caim and Angelus pursue the Grotesquerie Queen through a mysterious dimensional phenomenon. The battle eventually transports Caim and Angelus away from their own world and into another dimension.

They arrive in modern-day Tokyo in 2003.

The appearance of the dragon and the Grotesquerie Queen causes a confrontation with the Japanese military. Caim and Angelus fight through the sky above Tokyo, but the military eventually shoots down Angelus.

Angelus falls from the sky and dies, while the Grotesquerie Queen's remains release an unknown supernatural substance into the atmosphere.

This event creates the connection between Drakengard and the NieR universe. The supernatural particles eventually become connected to a devastating disease known as White Chlorination Syndrome.

White Chlorination Syndrome

After the events of Drakengard's Ending E, strange particles begin appearing in the atmosphere. Humanity initially has no understanding of what has happened.

The particles are connected to a supernatural phenomenon that causes humans who become infected to either die or enter a violent state of madness. This disease becomes known as White Chlorination Syndrome, or WCS.

The disease spreads throughout the human population and becomes one of the greatest threats humanity has ever faced.

Humanity eventually discovers that the disease is connected to the arrival of the supernatural matter from the Drakengard world. Researchers desperately search for a way to stop the disease.

This eventually leads to one of the most important scientific projects in the entire NieR universe.

Project Gestalt

Scientists create Project Gestalt, an enormous plan intended to preserve humanity.

The basic idea is to separate a person's soul, known as a Gestalt, from their physical body. The original body, called a Replicant, would then be created without the person's original consciousness.

The Gestalt would be preserved separately until humanity could eventually be restored.

However, there is a major problem. The Replicant bodies are not supposed to develop independent consciousness. They are intended to be empty biological vessels that will eventually receive their original Gestalts.

Instead, the Replicants gradually develop their own personalities, emotions and societies.

The Shadowlord

One particular human becomes extremely important to Project Gestalt. This man becomes known as the Shadowlord.

His Gestalt is unusually powerful and stable. His continued existence becomes essential to Project Gestalt because the Replicant system depends on his Gestalt remaining stable.

The Shadowlord's Replicant is a young boy who becomes known as NieR. The Shadowlord's daughter, Yonah, also becomes extremely important.

When Yonah becomes infected with the Black Scrawl, the Shadowlord agrees to become part of Project Gestalt in the hope that the plan will eventually allow humanity to survive.

However, the plan begins to fail. Gestalts begin to relapse into violent, unstable forms known as Shades.

NieR Replicant

Many years later, the world has become almost unrecognisable. Human civilisation has collapsed. Cities and buildings from the old world remain as ruins, while small Replicant communities have formed throughout the world.

The protagonist lives with his younger sister, Yonah, in a small village. Yonah suffers from the Black Scrawl, a mysterious disease that causes black markings to spread across the body.

NieR becomes determined to find a cure for her. He eventually discovers Grimoire Weiss, a mysterious talking book capable of using powerful magic.

NieR eventually meets Kainé, a warrior who has been possessed by a Shade, and Emil, a young boy with incredible magical powers.

Together, the group searches for the Sealed Verses while fighting the Shades that have appeared throughout the world.

The Truth About the Shades

As NieR's journey continues, the truth behind the Shades gradually becomes clear.

The creatures that NieR and his companions have been killing are not simply monsters. They are the Gestalts of the original humans.

The Replicants are the physical bodies created for those humans, while the Shades are the separated souls.

This means that the world of NieR is built upon a terrible misunderstanding. The Replicants believe that they are human, while the Gestalts believe that they are the original humans.

Both sides have been fighting for survival without fully understanding what the other side truly is.

The Shadowlord's Castle

Eventually, NieR discovers that the Shadowlord is responsible for much of what has happened to Yonah.

The Shadowlord's goal is to reunite with his Replicant body and restore his daughter.

NieR eventually reaches the Shadowlord's castle and confronts him. During the confrontation, the Shadowlord is defeated.

His Gestalt finally collapses. Without the Shadowlord's Gestalt, the remaining Gestalts can no longer be properly maintained. Project Gestalt effectively collapses.

The Gestalt Collapse

After the Shadowlord's defeat, the Replicants continue living while the original humans gradually disappear.

The Black Scrawl continues to spread, while the Shades become increasingly unstable.

The Replicants begin to disappear one by one as the biological system that supported their existence breaks down.

NieR successfully saved Yonah, but the price of doing so was effectively the end of the original human race.

Thousands of Years Later

The events of NieR Replicant take place thousands of years before NieR:Automata.

During this enormous period of time, the remaining Replicant civilisation eventually disappears. Humanity itself is already effectively extinct.

The world continues to decay, with nature reclaiming the ruins of human civilisation.

Eventually, a new conflict begins between two artificial populations: Androids and Machines.

The Alien Invasion

At some point in the distant future, aliens arrive on Earth and deploy armies of Machines.

The Machines successfully overwhelm the planet. The surviving humans escape to the Moon, while Android forces remain on Earth and continue fighting the Machines.

The Androids are given a single purpose: protect humanity and eventually reclaim Earth.

However, the humans on the Moon are already dead. Humanity no longer exists, but the Androids continue fighting for a species that has already disappeared.

The Machine Network

The Machines initially appear to be simple weapons controlled by the aliens. However, they gradually become more sophisticated.

They learn, communicate and develop emotions. They begin imitating human behaviour and attempting to understand humanity.

Eventually, the Machines become capable of questioning their own purpose.

The Androids were created to serve humanity, while the Machines were created to destroy humanity's enemies. Yet both groups begin developing the same emotions, desires and behaviours that once belonged to humans.

Project YoRHa

Eventually, the Androids create Project YoRHa.

YoRHa is designed to give Androids a renewed purpose and help them fight the Machines. YoRHa soldiers are more advanced than ordinary Androids and are organised into specialised combat units.

Two of the most famous YoRHa units are YoRHa No.2 Type B, better known as 2B, and YoRHa No.9 Type S, better known as 9S.

YoRHa soldiers are told that humanity survives on the Moon and that they are fighting to reclaim Earth for mankind.

However, the truth behind YoRHa is much darker. The entire system is built around maintaining the Androids' belief that humanity still exists.

The 14th Machine War

NieR:Automata takes place during the 14th Machine War.

2B and 9S are sent to Earth as YoRHa soldiers to investigate the Machines and support the resistance.

Earth is now a massive collection of abandoned cities, forests, deserts and ruins. Human civilisation has disappeared completely, but traces of it remain everywhere.

The Androids encounter Machines that behave strangely. Some Machines imitate humans, form families, attempt to understand religion, or recreate human art and social structures.

The longer 2B and 9S remain on Earth, the more difficult it becomes to view the Machines as simple enemies.

Adam and Eve

Eventually, the Machine network produces two highly advanced beings known as Adam and Eve.

Adam and Eve are humanoid Machines created from the Machine network. They become fascinated with humanity, particularly human concepts such as death, violence, knowledge and relationships.

Adam becomes obsessed with understanding human nature, while Eve becomes deeply attached to Adam.

Their relationship becomes one of the most important Machine relationships in the story.

2B and 9S

As 2B and 9S continue their missions, they develop a close relationship.

9S is curious and constantly attempts to understand the world around him. 2B is more emotionally restrained and focused on completing her missions.

However, 2B hides a major secret. She is actually an executioner model whose purpose is to eliminate 9S whenever he discovers forbidden information.

9S repeatedly discovers information about humanity and YoRHa. Whenever this happens, 2B is ordered to kill him and his memories are restored into a new body.

This cycle has happened repeatedly, meaning 2B knows that eventually she may have to kill 9S again.

The Death of Adam and Eve

The conflict with Adam and Eve eventually reaches a climax. Adam is killed by 2B and 9S.

Eve becomes overwhelmed by grief and rage. He connects himself to the Machine network and becomes corrupted, forcing 2B and 9S to fight him.

9S eventually kills Eve, but the experience has a major emotional impact on him.

The Truth About YoRHa

The YoRHa Bunker is eventually attacked by the Machine network. The attack exposes a horrifying secret.

YoRHa was not simply fighting for humanity. Its entire system was designed around maintaining the belief that humanity still existed.

Humanity is already extinct.

The Androids are fighting for an ideal rather than an actual population. The revelation destroys the foundation of YoRHa's purpose.

The Logic Virus

The Machine network eventually develops a method of infecting Androids with a logic virus.

2B is eventually infected. 9S attempts to save her, but her condition deteriorates.

2B asks 9S to kill her before the virus completely takes control. 9S is forced to kill her.

Her death becomes one of the most devastating moments of the story and causes 9S to become increasingly consumed by grief and anger.

A2

Another YoRHa unit, A2, becomes central to the final part of the story.

A2 is a prototype YoRHa model who was abandoned after the Pearl Harbor Descent. She is considered a traitor by YoRHa, although the truth is much more complicated.

A2 eventually meets 9S after 2B's death. The two become connected by 2B's final wishes and the collapse of YoRHa.

9S's Collapse

After 2B's death, 9S becomes increasingly obsessed with revenge.

He begins digging deeper into the Machine network and discovers even more horrifying truths.

The Machines have developed their own forms of life, society and consciousness.

9S becomes increasingly unstable as he searches for answers. Eventually, he begins fighting Machines without caring about their motives or identities.

The Tower

The final conflict leads to the mysterious Tower.

The Tower is connected to the Machine network and contains enormous amounts of data.

9S, A2 and the remaining Androids eventually become involved in the final battle surrounding it.

A2 eventually fights 9S in the game's final confrontation. Their battle represents the collapse of the old world and the end of YoRHa.

Ending E — A New Beginning

After the events of the final battle, the Pods refuse to accept the destruction of 2B, 9S and A2.

The Pods reconstruct their data and attempt to restore the three characters. The process is uncertain, but there is now a possibility that they may continue living.

Ending E ultimately leaves their future uncertain, but hopeful. Unlike many previous stories in the NieR universe, the conclusion does not simply end with destruction.

Instead, it suggests that even after countless cycles of suffering, there may still be the possibility of rebuilding.

The Story of NieR

The entire history of NieR can be viewed as a chain of consequences. A supernatural event from another world creates the conditions for humanity's destruction. Humanity attempts to survive through Project Gestalt, which creates the Replicants and eventually leads to the events of NieR Replicant.

Thousands of years later, Androids continue fighting in humanity's name while Machines develop their own identities and emotions. Project YoRHa creates another layer of lies to give the Androids purpose, eventually leading to the events of NieR:Automata.

The story repeatedly asks what happens when beings created for a specific purpose develop their own identities, emotions and desires.

From Caim and Angelus, to NieR, Kainé and Emil, and finally to 2B, 9S and A2, the world of NieR repeatedly explores the ideas of identity, humanity, suffering, purpose and free will.
