NieR:Automata & NieR:Replicant

Automata:

Earth was invaded by aliens, forcing surviving humans to flee to a lunar base. Humanity sends elite android soldiers of the YoRHa squad—primarily characters 2B, 9S, and A2—to reclaim the planet.

Replicant:

Set in a decaying, apocalyptic future where human civilization has regressed to medieval conditions, the story follows a kind young man. He embarks on a desperate quest to find a cure for his sister, Yonah, who is terminally ill with a mysterious disease called the Black Scrawl. Alongside a strange, talking magical book named Grimoire Weiss, he searches for the "Sealed verses" to save his sister, encountering unusual allies and dangerous shadow-like monsters known as shades along the way. The narrative explores themes of sacrifice, the nature of humanity, and the consequences of one's actions in a world filled with moral ambiguity.

YoRHa No.2 Type B 2B — Battle Android
2B is a YoRHa android and the main protagonist of NieR:Automata. She is calm, disciplined, and highly skilled in combat, fighting alongside 9S to protect humanity from the machines that have taken over Earth. Despite her serious personality, 2B develops a strong emotional connection with 9S as they uncover the truth behind the war and their own existence.
